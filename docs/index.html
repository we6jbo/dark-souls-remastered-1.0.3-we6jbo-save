What the project does

The simulator follows major high-level NG+ progression with original narration and simplified mechanics. It includes a Tkinter player GUI, locally displayed area maps, a text spectator feed, colored ASCII presentation, automatic computer play after two minutes of inactivity, five-minute pause screens, local ttyrec recording, and an optional public asciinema livestream.
